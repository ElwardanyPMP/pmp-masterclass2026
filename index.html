Course Curriculum

7 powerful modules covering everything you need for PMP success

Introduction to Project Management

Fundamentals of project management, the role of a project manager, and an overview of the PMP certification process and requirements.

Understanding organizational structures, enterprise environmental factors, and project integration management processes.
